Claude sounds like a person's name, but it's an AI helper. You can ask it a question, and it writes back. So who owns Claude? A company called Anthropic does.

A brother and sister named Dario and Daniela Amodei helped start Anthropic in 2021. They started it with a few other people. Dario is the CEO, and Daniela is the president. They help run the company. A whole team works on Claude and tries to make its answers more helpful and safer.

Does being the boss mean Dario owns Claude all by himself? Nope! Think about a company that makes a video game. One person might lead the team that makes the game. Lots of people can own little pieces of the company. Those pieces are called shares.

It works the same way with Claude. People can own shares of Anthropic, and Anthropic owns Claude.
