AI 10 years ago was completely different from what it is today. Back then, it was considered a cool tech flex; with new additions like Siri rocking the world for 5 years, it was the gold standard. But only 3 years after Apple's Siri came out, Amazon Alexa rocked and shocked the world. Then soon after the google assistant in 2016, then only 4 years later, GPT-3 came out again shocking the world. Do you sense a pattern here? Soon after every major AI breakthrough, another company, another AI, stood up and took all the fame. Now, look at today: the main models kids and students think of are ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Guess why? It is because they use these three AIs to help with their schoolwork or cheat. It is imperative that now more than ever that AI culture and practice need to change, at the rate we are going now AI could literally lessen the reach of the mind in coming generations, or it could even promote cognitive offloading. " They grew lazier, “resorting to copy-paste by session 3.”(How AI Affects Your Brain According to the Studies: A (Very Big) Compilation. The entire literature clearly points to a single surprising finding: Alberto Romero)

Cognitive offloading is the mental shortcut of using tools, your environment, or other people to reduce the amount of thinking and remembering your brain has to do. Now, from the quote earlier and the definition of cognitive offloading, think: if you stay in this state and rely on AI for too long, guess what happens to your brain? The gray matter literally shrinks. If that wasn't bad enough, your brain literally prunes your synapses. See, your body has a use-it-or-lose-it policy; for example, muscle growth happens when you do multiple sets of low- to high-intensity exercise, but if you suddenly stop using those muscles. Let's say you tear your bicep; it loses all that mass. The same applies to the brain. Now, through all this explaining, you are probably wondering what my point on all this COGNITIVE OFFLOADING stuff is. The point is that Companies need to take action; students whom we are trusting the future with rely on AI all the time, almost too much, so much so that if this continues, the minds in the generations to come will literally lessen.

When I say "AI could lessen the reach of the mind," what do you think of: a boy giving up on a math problem, or a girl struggling with a simple comprehension text? That is just the best of what could happen, not to say that it is any good. Your brain will literally shrink over generations if we keep going at this rate. Which is why we need to fight back against these companies; it has been far too long. Kids need to think. They need their minds to grow to nurture. Brilliant people like the great Isaac Newton or Srinivasa Ramanujan didn't have AI, and look at them; they had wonder. Being honest, if I lived during the time of Isaac Newton, the thought of a force pulling us to a larger object wouldn't even cross my mind, but to him it did.

Throughout this article, I have made it clear that AI can ruin the human brain. Which is why I believe we need to change the world of AI. Don't get me wrong, AI is a great tool helping us in the fields of medicine, but we should limit its reach to kids. Of course, it can teach them better, but you know what else it can do? It can help them cheat.
